## Track 1: 3D camera solve
Goal: scale ratio (push/pull), roll, and per-layer parallax for the camera moves already segmented in new-analysis-3/camera (n about 192 moves, 7 videos).
Method S1: feature tracks (LK or DIS) -> robust essential matrix / homography-plane decomposition per frame pair, chained, with bundle adjustment; scene = flat cards at depths. Method S2: a different principle, e.g. plane-sweep per layer with per-layer similarity transforms fitted to tracked points (RANSAC), then depth-ordered camera dolly estimated from the parallax of the layers. A third method S3 only if S1 and S2 agree under 75 %.
Known-answer test FIRST: at least 40 synthetic moves (push-in, pull-out, pan, tilt, roll, orbit; 3 layers at known depths; Rick-like sharpening and compression). Tool pass marks: scale ratio within 5 % on 90 %+ of moves; roll within 0.5 degrees on 90 %+; pan/tilt within 5 % of width/height on 90 %+; layer depth ranking correct on 90 %+. A tool that misses a pass mark is reported, not used for that field.
Real-data agreement (S1 vs S2, per move): scale ratio within 10 %; roll within 1.0 degree; pan and tilt within 10 % of the move's size or 0.5 % of frame width; layer ranking identical. Per-field agreement rates printed under this definition and under the v3/v4 camera tolerances of new-analysis-3 (camera/PREREG).
Outputs: per move scale ratio, roll, pan, tilt (camera-space), per-layer (far, character plane, near) shifts, parallax ratio near:far; per-video summary; recipe table update for RENDER_SPEC.

## Track 2: two-person hand-labelled actions
Items: 100 action windows drawn BEFORE labelling by a script with seed 2026112005 from the 7 videos: stratified by video (14 or 15 each), windows of 36 frames, at random start positions (not aligned to earlier logs), no window overlapping another by more than 6 frames, and 20 of the 100 inside shots where the camera is nearly still (from camera segmentation) so locked-camera labelling gets its own rate. Each window shown as every frame (1 frame per cell, counter printed, zoomed crop strips of the character region plus full frame).
Two labellers (separate agents, no shared context, blind to each other, to every earlier timing and to any tool output). Each lists every action in each window: type (snap, tween, redraw, cycle, walk, turn, sit, stand, throw, fall, impact, gesture, face change, mouth change, none), start frame (first frame that differs from the held pose), end frame (first frame of the new hold), and overshoot yes/no.
Pre-registered agreement: action found (same type class, time overlap) rate; start and end within +-1 f, +-2 f, +-3 f; travel within +-2 f; overshoot yes/no match. Pass marks: RULE 99 % at +-2 f, GUIDANCE 75 % at +-2 f. If under 75 % at +-2 f, report the +-3 f and +-4 f rates labelled post-hoc and the third method (adjudicator me on disputed items) separately; a field under 75 % at +-2 f stays UNCERTAIN as a window-free number.
Adjudication: for items the two disagree on by more than 2 f, a third blind labeller (a third agent) labels them; consensus = median of three; consensus values are published only for items where at least two of three agree within 2 f.
Models: Opus builds packs and the camera tools; Sonnet labellers (two, different seeds of prompt wording), a third Sonnet for disputes. State what ran.
