# Design vocabulary (owner's mandatory list, Nov 17 2026) — record a value or "none/not used" for EVERY term, per shot and per on-screen graphic. Give numbers wherever a measure exists. Add any property found that is not listed under "EXTRA".

T  Typography: typeface+family, classification (serif/sans/slab/script/display/hand-lettered), weight, width, italic/oblique, case, size+hierarchy (cap height px @1080w and % of frame H), TRACKING (letter-spacing in % of font size, per text type, animates?), kerning, leading (line height), word spacing, baseline + baseline shift, alignment, line length + line breaks, rag, orphans/widows, ligatures, glyph variants, stroke/outline width+colour, fill, gradient fill, inner/outer glow, drop shadow (offset/blur/colour/opacity), bevel/emboss, extrusion/3D text, text on a path, distortion/warp, mask/knockout, word emphasis method.
LM Lettering motion: reveal style (typewriter/word-by-word/letter/pop/slide/wipe/scale/hard swap), entrance+exit timing in frames, easing, overshoot, stagger, hold time, sync to VO word (frames early/late), safe-area placement (x,y % of frame), reading time per word.
C  Colour: palette (hex), dominant/secondary/accent %, hue, saturation, value, contrast ratio, temperature, harmony, grade/LUT feel, black level, highlight rolloff, tint, vibrance, per-scene palette shift, gradients (type/angle/stops).
S  Shape+line: shape language, line weight + consistency (px @1080w), outline colour, outline variation, stroke taper, corner radius, silhouette readability, negative space, texture (paper/grain/halftone/brush/noise/hatching/stippling).
CL Composition+layout: frame size/aspect, grid, margins, safe areas, thirds, golden ratio, focal point, hierarchy, balance, alignment, proximity, repetition, rhythm, white space, scale/proportion, depth layers (FG/MG/BG), overlap, framing, leading lines, cropping, bleed, head-room/look-room.
DL Depth+lighting: key/fill/rim direction+colour, falloff, shadow shape+softness, contact shadows, AO, highlights/specular, bloom/glow, vignette, DOF + focal plane, blur type+amount, haze, parallax speed per layer, film grain.
CM Camera+tracking: position, lens feel (wide/tele, est. mm), push-in/pull-out, pan, tilt, truck, dolly, crane, orbit, handheld, roll, tracking shot, motion tracking (graphic locked to object, lag/ease), speed ramp, zoom, punch-in.
MD Motion design: keyframe spacing, easing (in/out/bezier/spring), anticipation, follow-through, overshoot, squash+stretch, secondary motion, smear/motion blur, hold, loop, stagger, mask reveal, wipe, match cut, morph, dissolve, whip, shape-matched transition — each with exact duration in frames.
CH Character design: head-to-body ratio, face shape, eye size+spacing, brow, mouth shapes (viseme set), face-swap cadence (frames), blink timing, pose language, outline weight, costume colour-blocking, prop scale.
IA Imagery+assets: illustration/photo/3D, painted backdrops, texture maps, icons, pictograms, maps, documents/props in-world, logos/marks, poster treatment, mockup/frame style.
AV Audio-visual sync: cut-to-beat, hit points, SFX-to-motion sync, music swells+ducking, silence moments.
EXTRA: anything else observed.
