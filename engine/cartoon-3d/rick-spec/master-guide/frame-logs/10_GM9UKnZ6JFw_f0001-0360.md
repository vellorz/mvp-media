## Rules derived

1. **Open on motion, not a hold.** The camera is already pushing at frame 1 and dives into the subject within 1 s (10_GM9UKnZ6JFw f1-30).
2. **Use a story object as the camera's guide rail.** The camera tracks the bell string from the finger up to the bell, so the move explains the mechanism (f49-92). Later the same string is the anchor of both dissolves (f97-106, f299-309).
3. **Join scenes with shape-matched dissolves of ~10 f, not cuts.** Overlay identical geometry (post plus string plus bell) at the same screen position and keep the camera moving through the blend (f97-106, f299-309). Zero hard cuts in the first 12 s.
4. **Switch rendering style for the explainer beat.** 3D world → flat 2D cut-away diagram framed by a window, then back to 3D (f97 → f299). Keep one recurring prop as the continuity anchor.
5. **Keep a reaction face in every explainer frame.** A background onlooker (nightcap neighbour) is visible during the diagram, then defocused once the main character takes over the acting (f122-157).
6. **Ease every camera move in and out over ~30-45 f, and land the settle on the phrase end.** Dive lands on "alive" (f28); string reaches the bell on "finger." (f79-92); push-in reaches the finger on "finger." (f285-288).
7. **Use one-word, hard-swap captions locked to VO onsets within ±1 f** (34 of 38 captions within ±1 f; max +3). There is no caption animation of any kind (f2-360).
8. **Caption spec:** white black-weight grotesque, ~77 px (cap 55 px), 4-5 px black stroke plus small down-right shadow, centred, baseline at 71.6% H. Max 3 words, and function words ride with the next word (f20 "a man", f156 "up in a", f274 "the bodies").
