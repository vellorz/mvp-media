## Derived rules

1. Join scenes with 13-14 f cross-dissolves, never hard cuts, inside a story sequence (10_GM9UKnZ6JFw f394-407, f514-526, f562-575, f655-667, f710-722).
2. Bridge each dissolve with a matching shape or position: lit window -> bedroom window behind the head (f394-407); window mullions -> street (f514-526); right porch with figures -> porch interior (f562-575); doctor lying centre-bottom -> doctor sitting centre-bottom (f710-722).
3. Alternate cool-blue exterior and warm candle/lantern interior on every dissolve so each scene change reads as a temperature flip (metrics lab_b f391 -53 -> f421 +5 -> f541 -52 -> f601 +8 -> f691 -48).
4. Keep the camera always moving slowly (push 5-12 % per shot), and save a fast ease-in push (~2.6x in 30 f) for the tension beat, ending as a push-through a window (f481-513 "rings faster").
5. Show picture cause before the line: the window lights up ~4 f before "The guy" (f381 vs VO f385); porch lights stagger on across "is out on their porches" (f535-552).
6. Captions: single line, 1-3 words, hard swap with no animation, centred x 540, baseline y 1376, white Verdana-Bold-like with ~6 px black stroke + hard 3 px shadow; on screen at the word's onset (0 to +2 f early); caption stays crisp above dissolves (f394-407, f562-575).
7. Narrator-quoted dialogue is lip-flapped by characters with mouth swaps every 1-3 f (man f584-594, woman f611-633), while non-speakers react with eyes only (sweat drop, half-lid side glances f614-616).
8. Tell the gag with a three-step face beat: reason (squint f416-437) -> relief (closed-eye smile f445-482) -> one-eye snap open on the escalation word (f484 "faster").
9. Use one-point perspective wides (vanishing point ~x50 % y33 %) for "whole town" beats, with the empty road as the caption zone (f514-575).
