# Frame log: 14_VS1Z_Q-Klt8, frames 385-768

- **Video:** 14_VS1Z_Q-Klt8 (dragon video: bronze earthquake jar with eight dragons, day/court story, 64.9 s), 1080x1920, 30 fps.
- **Range:** f385-f768 = 12.80 s to 25.57 s (384 frames, 12.8 s).
- **Method:** I viewed all 384 frames on the 16 contact sheets from sheet_f0385-0408 to sheet_f0745-0768. To classify the transitions at the edges, I also checked context frames on sheet_f0361-0384 and sheet_f0769-0792. I opened 540-wide singles to read faces (f415-f529 emperor, f566-f600 scientist, f679-f721 courtiers, f724-f768 emperor).
- **Full-res measurements:** I grabbed 1080x1920 frames at f400, f450, f470, f520, f560, f585, f600, f625, f660, f700, f730, f740 and f760, and sampled them with PIL.
- **Motion data:** I computed a per-frame mean-abs diff myself, because the metrics CSV was not present when I started. I measured camera motion with OpenCV phase correlation (translation) and ORB plus a partial-affine fit (scale per 2-6 frames).
- **Dissolve curves:** I fitted the dissolve opacity curves by least-squares projection of each frame onto (outgoing frame, incoming frame).
- **Transcript:** The words.tsv gives the word starts. One ASR error: the TSV has "mind." at 21.76 s, but the caption and the picture both say "wine.".
- **Frame and time:** frame n = (n-1)/30 s. The VO frame for a word is t*30+1.
