## Derived rules
1. Bridge every scene change with a 4-7 frame dissolve, never a hard cut, inside narrative runs (14_VS1Z_Q-Klt8 f806-809, f890-896, f952-958, f992-997, f1116-1120).
2. Prefer a silhouette match across the dissolve: round head → round arch (f806-809), jar → jar (f890-896), jar dome → arched portcullis (f1116-1120).
3. Land the character's face swap on the first frame of the outgoing dissolve so the reaction carries across (emperor grin f806).
4. Captions are 1 word, or 2 when the words are short or form one unit ("the city" f842, "all eight." f1071). Hard swap with no animation, centred at 71.7 % H, within ±1-2f of the VO (mean +0.6f; f770-1152).
5. Captions sit above the dissolve layer and are never faded with the picture (f806-809, f890-896, f992-997).
6. Use camera reveals in stages to pace a list of escalating beats: each pull-out stage widens just before the next "ball drop" line (f1010-1058 for "a ball dropped", f1058-1100 for "then all eight"; total 3.7x).
7. Change the grade hard with the story clock: warm red court (luma 80) → bright day (150) → sunset (132, highest contrast) → night blue (93-109, highest saturation). The day-to-night jump happens inside a 6f dissolve (f992-997).
8. Fake travel with layer parallax rather than moving the camera: the hill slides down-left under static characters and sky (f897-951).
9. A two-beat move inside one shot (pull-out then push-in) refocuses attention on a new subject (gate shot f810-840 out, f840-889 in on the jar for "jar and all").
10. Mix 2D outlined cartoon characters with outline-free 3D-rendered props (bronze dragons and toads, f997-1116). The character keeps a thick ink outline (~6 % of head width) so it separates from the 3D.
11. Escalate fright with anticipation then a pose jump: eyes shut + gritted teeth f1083 → standing + hair spikes f1085, with a crow burst f1083-1092 as a secondary-motion accent.
12. Blinks are rare and 3f long (f1067-1069, f1097-1099). Expression swaps carry the acting, not in-betweens.
