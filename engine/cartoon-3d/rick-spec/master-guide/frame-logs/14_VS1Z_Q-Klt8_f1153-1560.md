- Range: f1153-1560, 408 frames, 13.6 s.
- Method: I viewed all 408 frames on contact sheets sheet_f1153-1176 through sheet_f1537-1560 (17 sheets). For context at the edges I also viewed sheet_f1129-1152 and sheet_f1561-1584. I opened singles at 540w for f1169/1170 and f1256-1259. I pulled 13 full-res frames (f1160, 1200, 1230, 1258, 1290, 1330, 1380, 1400, 1425, 1445, 1470, 1520, 1556) for px measures and hex values. Every "diff" and "luma" value in this log comes from that computation. The VO times come from words.tsv, and a word at t falls on frame t*30+1.
- Frame/time: frame n = (n-1)/30 s.
