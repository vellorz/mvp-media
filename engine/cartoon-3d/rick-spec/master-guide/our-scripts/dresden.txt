In 1730, a king ordered the biggest cake in the world. Augustus the Strong threw a party for 30,000 soldiers, and royals from across Europe came to watch. So 100 bakers got to work, 3,600 eggs, over 300 churns of milk, a ton of flour. The stollen came out seven meters long and nearly two tons heavy. No oven in Saxony could hold it, so the king's architect built a brand new one. But how do you cut a two-ton cake? With a knife as tall as a man! 24,000 slices, enough for the whole camp. And today, every December, Dresden's Christmas market, running since 1434, still parades a giant stollen through the old town. Dresden, on your wall.
