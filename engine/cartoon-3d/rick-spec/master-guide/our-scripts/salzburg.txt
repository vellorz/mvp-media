The most famous Christmas song on earth was first played on a guitar. Christmas Eve, 1818, a tiny church in Oberndorf, just outside Salzburg. A young priest, Joseph Moore, had a poem he'd written two years before. He walked through the snow to the village schoolteacher Franz Gruber and asked him for a melody. Gruber wrote it that same day. That night, by candlelight, they sang it together. Moore on guitar, Gruber singing bass, the choir joining every chorus. Later, an organ builder working on the church organ heard it and carried it home over the mountains. Traveling singers took it across Europe, then all the way to New York. Today, Silent Night is sung in more than 300 languages. And it all began next to Salzburg. Salzburg, on your wall.
