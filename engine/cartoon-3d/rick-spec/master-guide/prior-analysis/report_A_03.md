## 2. Visual production
- Backgrounds (OBSERVED): stylised low-poly / painterly real-time 3D — banded canyon strata, bloom, DOF, volumetric smoke, motion blur, particle sparks; fully built sets (city, domed station, trestles, carriage interiors). INFERRED: Unreal Engine 5 (or Unity) with stylised packs (Synty "POLYGON Western" look). Not Minecraft/Cities/Planet Coaster.
- Characters (OBSERVED): round white heads, thick dark outlines, dot/line eyes, costume detail on rounded torsos, thin black line limbs. They cast ground shadows (f60–90), are occluded by the train, keep correct perspective through orbits and the boss bend (f426–465) → INFERRED toon-shaded 3D rigs with outline shader. Animation = puppet poses + expression swaps every 0.3–0.7 s (smug, wide eyes, angry, gritted teeth, wink f336, sweat drop f1005). Mouth shapes only on dialogue (f540–590); no lip-sync on narrator lines.
- Gags/FX (OBSERVED): sparks (f16, f768), cap blown off by train (f98), purple liquid-fill wipe on face (f513–521) + cartoon steam puffs (f523–600), coffee spill (f884–893).
- No 2D arrows, diagrams or freeze-frames — all exposition is diegetic signage.
