title: Weekly scripts now ride platform trends and tell a true story about each poster's place

## What changed (owner request, Oct 29)
The Sunday marketing run has a new step, 2b, between SEO research and the Gate 1 scripts doc. It applies from Sun Nov 1 (week of Nov 2-8). Nothing else in the workflow changes:
- Sunday is still data, research and the scripts doc only.
- Building starts when the owner says "finish this week".
- Gate 2 still comes before anything is scheduled.

## How step 2b works
1. **Trends, per platform.** Check this week's trending captions, caption patterns, hashtags/tags, hook formats and story formats on TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts. Also check Pinterest Trends for seasonal search interest.
   - Sources are public: TikTok Creative Center, YouTube search suggestions and top Shorts, Reels and hashtag pages, and web search.
   - Every trend gets a date and a source. Anything that couldn't be checked is marked [unverified].
2. **Filter.** Keep only trends that fit vintage travel and the real buyers (travel-loving homeowners, gift buyers).
   - Tags are for buyers only. No seller or follow-for-follow tags.
   - No trending songs. All music stays original.
3. **A story for each video.** Pick the poster(s) first, then build the script on a true, sourced story about that location. Possible angles are local history, a legend, a film or TV location, an event happening that week, or a little-known fact.
   - Where a trending hook or caption format fits, shape the story to it.
   - Write the caption and tags separately for each platform, to match that platform's trends.
   - Never invent a story. Every fact needs a source.
4. **Output.**
   - Trend findings go into the week's analytics.md.
   - In the scripts doc, each video gets a "Trend + story" line: which trend or format it uses, the story, and its source.
   - The weekly handoff lists the trends used and each video's story.

## Learning loop
lessons.md will track which trends and story types (history, legend, film location, event, hidden fact) earn views, watch time and listing visits. Later weeks lean on the winners. Orders stay the north star.

- Flag any Etsy listing whose title or tags could pick up a trending search term. The owner handles listing SEO through Hermes.
