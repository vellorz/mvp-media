## What changed (owner approved Nov 2, 2026)
- **Two runs a week instead of one.** Sunday 12:00 CT plans Mon-Thu (run A). Thursday 12:00 CT plans Fri-Sun (run B). Each run does a data check-in, research, a Gate 1 doc (format menu + scripts), then build, Gate 2 and scheduling after the owner says "finish this run". Both gates stay mandatory.
- **Volume: 2 posts per day on every platform** (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest), about 14 unique videos a week, up from about 6. YouTube is capped at 2 a day by the owner.
- **First Thursday run: Nov 5.** It adds 4 videos and 3 pins so Fri Nov 6-Sun Nov 8 reach 2 a day. Full system from the week of Nov 9.
- **Schedulers:** Buffer for IG/FB/TikTok videos (10 queued per channel). YouTube Shorts now go through YouTube Studio's own scheduler on the owner's PC (Remote Control), not Metricool. Metricool is TikTok carousels only (20/month). Pinterest stays native on the owner's PC.
- **Variety and learning:** every run picks from a format library (paper collage, map route, "3 things you didn't know", then-and-now, room reveal, Mr. Hale, silkscreen, kinetic poem, which-would-you-hang, postcard from 1930, trend slot): at least 4 formats per run, never the same format twice in a row. A new file, `hermes/insights/format-scoreboard.md`, scores every format (and voice) per platform. Promote after beating the median 2 runs in a row; retire after trailing it 2 runs. Promotions and retirements go to lessons.md.
- **Voice:** the owner upgraded ElevenLabs to Starter (commercial license). Default narrator is now **Drew** (casual, curious travel voice). Max, Samara X and Mr. Hale's voice stay in test slots on the scoreboard.

## File and naming changes in the shared brain (vellorz/mvp-media)
