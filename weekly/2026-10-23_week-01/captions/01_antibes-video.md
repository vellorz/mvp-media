## Fact check
- Late 1912 and early 1913: Braque and Picasso made papiers collés (pasted paper). Source: [Tate, papier collé](https://tate.org.uk/art/art-terms/p/papier-colle).
- 1946: offered part of Château Grimaldi as a studio; worked there two months in autumn; left 23 paintings and 44 drawings; on 26 Dec 1966 it became the Picasso Museum, "the first ever museum dedicated to the artist". Source: [Antibes Juan-les-Pins tourism](https://antibesjuanlespins.com/en/discover/the-must-sees/picasso-museum).
