You don't need any setup for Metricool or Buffer reminders. **At 17:55 Central on each video day, I'll message you here in the Claude app**, so make sure the app's notifications are on. The message will have:

## On each video day

### A. TikTok (from my 17:55 message)

1. Tap the **video link** in my message and save the video to your phone (Share → Save video, or Download).
2. **Copy the caption** from my message.
3. Open TikTok, tap **+** and choose the video from your gallery.
4. Tap **Add sound**, search for the song and select it. TikTok picks a clip of the song for you. Keep it unless you'd like a different part.
5. Tap **Volume**. Set **Original sound to 100** (that's Mr. Hale and the paper sounds) and **Added sound to about 15–20**. The voice must stay clearly on top.
6. Tap **Next** and **paste** the caption into the description box. The hashtags are already in it.
7. Check that the cover frame looks good, then tap **Post** at 18:00.

### B. YouTube Short (right after TikTok, same video)

1. Open the **YouTube** app and tap **+ → Create a Short** (or **Upload a video**).
2. Pick the **same video** from your gallery. It's already there from step A1.
3. Tap **Add sound**, search for the same song, and select it.
4. Tap **Volume**. Keep **Original sound** high and set the **added sound** low (about 15–20%), just as on TikTok.
5. Tap **Next**. For the **title**, copy the "YouTube Short title" line from that video's `post.md`, which is listed below. Set **Visibility** to **Public**, set **Audience** to **"No, it's not made for kids"**, then tap **Upload Short**.

If the song isn't offered in YouTube's library, choose the closest track it suggests or post with just the original sound. Hale's voice and the sound effects already carry the video.
